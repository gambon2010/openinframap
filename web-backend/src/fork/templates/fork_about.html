Using it

  • Search (top): names of substations, plants, places, sites; coordinates (47.75, 7.31); military grid references (32T LT 725 899). Where the cursor is, with its grid reference: bottom right (click to copy).
  • Click anything on the map for its details; from a substation, a route over the grid; from a line, where it goes.
  • Layers and Key (buttons on the right): what's shown, and what the colours mean. Status board (⚠): damage and threats, and the power supply mode.
  • My layers: your own layers, drawn with the toolbar at the top. Keys: V select, S symbol, A graphic, L line, R area, M marker, P picture, C copy from the map, D measure a distance, E an area, O range rings; Enter or a double-click finishes, Backspace takes back a point, right-click starts again, Esc stops; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo; Delete deletes what's selected. Changes are shared once you Save.
  • settings (top): the background map, satellite imagery, address search; your account (admins: everyone's). print: the map as a PDF with a legend.
